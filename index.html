The Studio

Built one brand at a time

I started Himadri Raj Graphics from my room in West Bengal with a laptop, a design tablet gifted by my elder brother, and a folder full of half-finished practice files. There was no big studio, no office — just late nights getting one logo, one poster, one post right before moving to the next.

Every brief still gets the same treatment: I sit with what the client actually needs — their colours, their audience, their tone — before I open a blank canvas. A design that looks good but doesn't fit the brand isn't a finished job to me, it's a draft.

What started as freelance practice has grown into a small, dependable design studio that local businesses and creators now message directly on WhatsApp when they need something made properly.
